Better Gemini gives Google Gemini the power-user upgrades it's missing — a wider chat layout, address-bar quick launch, one-click chat export, and 20+ keyboard shortcuts. Everything runs in your browser. No accounts, no tracking, and the add-on sends nothing to the developer or any third party.

Features
- Wide chat layout — expands the conversation to about 98% of the window so you stop reading Gemini through a narrow column.
- Quick launch from the address bar — type "gem", then your prompt, and press Enter to jump straight into Gemini with the prompt already sent. Open it in the current tab, a new tab, or quietly in the background.
- Export full chat — one click saves or copies the entire conversation as a Markdown file.
- 20+ keyboard shortcuts — create, delete, and navigate chats, edit, copy, and share without leaving the keyboard. Press the help hotkey for the full list.
- Default model and thinking level — automatically selects your preferred Gemini model and thinking level on every new chat.

Wide chat, export, keyboard shortcuts, and the default model each have a toggle — turn on only what you want from the popup or the options page.

Privacy first
Better Gemini runs only on gemini.google.com. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party data collection — the add-on declares "no" data collection to Firefox. It uses your own Gemini session. Chat text is read inside the page only when you use the export feature, and it is never stored or sent anywhere. Your settings (feature switches, preferred model, thinking level) are saved with the browser's storage API and may sync across your signed-in browsers through the browser's storage sync.

Who it's for
Anyone who lives in Gemini and wants a faster, wider, keyboard-driven experience with easy exporting — researchers, writers, developers, and power users.
